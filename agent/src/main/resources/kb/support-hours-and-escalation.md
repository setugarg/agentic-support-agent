# Support Hours and Escalation

## Support hours
Human support specialists are available Monday to Friday, 8am to 8pm Eastern time, and Saturday 9am to 5pm Eastern. The AI assistant is available 24/7.

## When the assistant escalates to a human
The assistant creates a support ticket for a human specialist when: a refund needs approval, a warranty claim is requested, a partial refund is requested, the customer is unhappy with the answer, or the request is outside what the assistant can do.

## Ticket priorities
High priority: safety issues, lost packages, or a customer who has contacted us 3 or more times about the same problem. Normal priority: everything else. High priority tickets are answered within 4 business hours; normal within 1 business day.
