# Returns and Refunds Policy

## Refund eligibility window
Delivered orders can be refunded within 30 days of the delivery date. The 30-day window starts on the day the carrier marks the package as delivered, not the order date. Orders delivered more than 30 days ago are not eligible for a refund, but may qualify for a warranty claim if the item is defective.

## Refund approval limits
Refunds of $100.00 or less are approved automatically and issued to the original payment method. Refunds over $100.00 require approval from a human support specialist; the order is placed in "refund pending approval" status and a specialist reviews it within 1 business day.

## How refunds are paid
Refunds go back to the original payment method. Card refunds take 5-10 business days to appear depending on the bank. We do not issue refunds as cash, wire transfer, or to a different card.

## Non-refundable items
Digital gift cards, e-gift cards and downloadable trail maps are non-refundable once delivered. Clearance items marked "final sale" are non-refundable.

## Orders that have not been delivered
Orders that are still processing can be cancelled instead of refunded. Orders that are already shipped cannot be refunded until they are delivered; customers can refuse the delivery or return it after it arrives.

## Partial refunds
We only issue full-order refunds through self-service. Partial refunds (for one item of a multi-item order) require a support ticket.
