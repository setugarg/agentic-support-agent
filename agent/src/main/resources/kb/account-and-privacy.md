# Account, Security and Privacy

## Order privacy
For privacy and security, support can only discuss orders that belong to the signed-in customer. We never share order details, addresses or payment information about another person's account, even if the requester provides the order number.

## Changing account email or address
Customers can change their email, password and saved addresses in Account Settings. Support agents cannot change account email addresses over chat.

## Payment information
Never share full card numbers, CVV codes or passwords in chat. Support will never ask for them.

## Data deletion requests
Customers can request deletion of their account data. Deletion requests are handled by the privacy team via a support ticket and completed within 30 days.
