# Shipping Policy

## Shipping speeds and costs
Standard shipping takes 3-7 business days and is free on orders over $50. Expedited shipping takes 2-3 business days and costs $12.95. Overnight shipping is available for $29.95 on orders placed before 1pm Eastern time.

## Carriers
We ship with UPS, USPS and FedEx. Digital products such as gift cards are delivered by email.

## Tracking a package
A tracking number is emailed when the order ships. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show movement after the label is created.

## Lost or late packages
If a package shows no tracking movement for 5 business days, or is more than 5 business days past its estimated delivery date, we open a carrier investigation. If the carrier confirms the package is lost we ship a free replacement or issue a full refund.

## International shipping
We currently ship only to the United States and Canada. Canadian orders may be subject to duties and taxes collected by the carrier.
