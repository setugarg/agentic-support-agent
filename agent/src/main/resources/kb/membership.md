# Summit Rewards Membership

## Earning points
Summit Rewards members earn 1 point per dollar spent. Points are credited 30 days after delivery, once the return window closes.

## Redeeming points
Every 100 points can be redeemed for a $5 reward certificate at checkout. Reward certificates expire 90 days after they are issued.

## Member benefits
Members get free standard shipping on every order, early access to sales, and two free expedited shipping upgrades per year.

## Joining
Membership is free. Customers can join from the Account page.
