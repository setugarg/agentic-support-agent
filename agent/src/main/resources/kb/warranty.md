# Warranty Policy

## Summit Gear lifetime warranty
Summit Gear branded products carry a lifetime warranty against defects in materials and workmanship. The warranty does not cover normal wear and tear, accidents, or damage from improper use.

## Third-party brand warranty
Products from other brands are covered by the manufacturer's warranty, typically 1 to 2 years. We help customers file the claim with the manufacturer.

## Tents and trekking poles
Broken tent poles and trekking pole sections are replaced free of charge under warranty within 2 years of purchase. Torn tent fabric is repaired for a flat $25 fee.

## How to make a warranty claim
Warranty claims are handled by a human specialist. Open a support ticket with the order number, a description of the defect and photos. Claims are reviewed within 3 business days.
